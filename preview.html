What we make

Ink on everything.

Design and production are the same conversation here. Nothing gets handed over a wall, which is why nothing comes back wrong.

  • SERVICE OFFSETStack of freshly cut sheets, edge on, so the layers read as lines. Neutral background, hard light, strong shadow.
    01

    Offset printing

    Long runs with the colour held exactly where you signed it off. Menus, brochures, stationery, publications.

  • SERVICE LARGEInstalled vinyl or a banner in a real Cayman location — a hotel lobby, a restaurant window. Shot straight on, daylight.
    02

    Large format

    Banners, window graphics, vehicle wraps and event build. Printed, finished and installed across the island.

  • SERVICE SCREENThe squeegee mid-pull across the screen, hands in frame. Shoot a burst and pick the frame where the ink is spreading.
    03

    Screen printing

    Uniforms, merchandise, tote bags and staff kit. Ink that survives the salt, the sun and the wash.

  • SERVICE FINISHINGDetail of hot foil or embossing catching the light. Tilt the stock until the foil flares. Dark background so the shine separates.
    04

    Foil & finishing

    Hot foil, emboss, deboss, spot UV, die-cutting. The part guests notice before they read a word.

  • SERVICE PACKAGINGA flat, unfolded die-cut box shot dead overhead on white, plus the same box assembled. Two frames, same lighting.
    05

    Packaging

    Structural design through to production. Boxes, sleeves, labels and bags that make the product feel worth it.

  • SERVICE SIGNAGECut acrylic or dimensional letters mounted on a wall, raking light so the letters throw shadows.
    06

    Signage

    Wayfinding, dimensional letters, acrylic and metal. Specified, fabricated and mounted.

Not sure which one you need? That is what the first call is for.

How it works

From file to finish.

Five stations. A job does not skip any of them, and nothing leaves the building that has not cleared all five.

  1. 01

    Before anything opens

    Brief

    We start with the finished object, not the file. Quantity, stock, budget, deadline, and where it will actually live once it leaves here.

  2. 02

    Artwork & prepress

    Design

    Artwork built by people who know what the press will do with it. No surprises at plate stage, no last-minute redraws.

  3. 03

    On the real stock

    Proof

    You see it on the real stock before we commit. Sign off on something you can hold, not a PDF on a screen.

  4. 04

    Colour held through the run

    Press

    Colour matched and checked through the run. If a sheet drifts, it does not leave the building.

  5. 05

    Out the door

    Finish

    Cut, folded, foiled, bound, packed. Delivered to the property, the restaurant or the stand, on the day it was promised.

The studio

From Barcelona
to Grand Cayman.

Magenta is led by Bárbara, a graphic designer who learned print the long way round: on the floor of production houses in Barcelona, where a job is judged by the object that comes out at the end rather than the file that went in.

That is still how the studio runs. Design and production sit in the same room, which means the person drawing the artwork already knows what the stock, the ink and the finish are going to do with it.

Today Magenta prints for a long list of the island's hotels, restaurants and retailers, and works as the production partner behind several of Cayman's creative agencies.
